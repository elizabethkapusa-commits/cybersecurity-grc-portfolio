# CloudShield Technologies: Cybersecurity GRC Portfolio

**Prepared by Elizabeth Kapusa**

CloudShield Technologies is a fictional SaaS organization created for an
independent cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance portfolio
project. The project demonstrates practical work across risk assessment,
NIST CSF 2.0 alignment, control testing, policy and standards
development, remediation planning, third-party risk, identity and access
governance, vulnerability management, and backup/recovery testing.

## Start Here

Begin with
**01_Executive_Report/CloudShield_Executive_GRC_Assessment_Report.docx**.
It summarizes the environment, assessment method, five priority risks,
NIST CSF posture, control-testing results, remediation roadmap, and
management recommendations.

## Portfolio Structure

### 01- Executive Report

A management-level assessment tying the full project together.

### 02- Risk and NIST Workbook

Risk register, risk matrix, evidence tracking, NIST CSF
current-vs-target assessment, and executive dashboard.

### 03- Policies and Standards

-   Access Control & Identity Management Policy
-   Vulnerability Management & Remediation Standard

### 04- Control Assessments

-   Privileged Access & MFA Control Assessment
-   Backup Restoration Control Test Report

### 05- Third-Party Risk

Vendor security risk assessment demonstrating due diligence, evidence
review, findings, conditional approval, remediation requirements, and
reassessment.

## Key Risk Scenarios

-   **GRC-001:** Inadequate privileged-account offboarding
-   **GRC-002:** Incomplete MFA enforcement and privileged identity
    design
-   **GRC-003:** Overdue production vulnerability and missing
    remediation SLA
-   **GRC-004:** Inadequate backup restoration testing
-   **GRC-005:** Inadequate third-party security review

## Skills Demonstrated

Risk identification and scoring; NIST CSF 2.0 assessment; security
control design and testing; IAM and privileged-access governance; MFA
and workload identity analysis; vulnerability remediation governance;
exception and risk-acceptance processes; backup and recovery control
testing; RTO/RPO analysis; third-party risk assessment; evidence
collection; remediation tracking; policy writing; executive reporting.

## Frameworks and Concepts

The project uses NIST CSF 2.0 as the primary organizational framework
and selected NIST SP 800-53 control concepts as a control reference. The
internal 1-4 maturity scale used in the workbook is a
portfolio-specific assessment scale and is not presented as an official
NIST Implementation Tier.

## Important Note

CloudShield Technologies, DataFlow Analytics, account names, employees,
systems, events, test results, and vendor scenarios in this repository
